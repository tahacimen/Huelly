```

## Short description (max 80 characters)

```
*(75 characters)*

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
A color code is falling. Memorize it before it lands, type it back — match it and it fires automatically.

Huelly is the kind of reflex-and-memory game you learn in one tap and spend years mastering. The rules take three seconds: a strip of colored blocks drops from the top of the screen, and you enter the same sequence from the palette below. Get it right and the strip blasts off, banking the points. Let it hit the floor and you lose a life.

The higher you catch it, the more it scores. Rushing always pays — and always makes you panic.

◆ 33 LEVELS
Sequences grow from short to long, colors from few to many. Every level builds on the last.

◆ 4 DIFFICULTIES, 4 SCORE MULTIPLIERS
Slow (×1) · Medium (×2) · Fast (×3) · Extreme (×5)
On Extreme the color buttons swap places after every strip — muscle memory won't save you, you actually have to look.

◆ SPEED MULTIPLIER
Push from 1× to 3× mid-run. The strip falls faster and the points scale with it. How brave are you?

◆ GLOBAL LEADERBOARD
Your total score goes on the worldwide board. All it takes is a nickname.

◆ NO ADS
No ads, no purchases, no subscriptions. You open it, you play.

◆ WORKS OFFLINE
Play without a connection; your progress stays on your device and syncs to the leaderboard when you're back online.

No account, no email, no permissions to accept. Just pick a nickname and start.
```

## Veri güvenliği formu (Play Console)

Play Console'daki "Data safety" bölümünde beyan edilecekler:

| Soru | Cevap |
|------|-------|
| Veri topluyor mu? | **Evet** |
| Toplanan veri | Kullanıcının seçtiği **takma ad** ve **toplam skor** |
| Kategori | "Kişisel bilgiler → Ad" (takma ad), "Uygulama etkinliği → Uygulama içi eylemler" (skor) |
| Aktarım şifreli mi? | **Evet** (HTTPS) |
| Kullanıcı silinmesini isteyebilir mi? | **Evet** (gizlilik politikasındaki adres üzerinden) |
| Veri paylaşılıyor mu (3. taraflara)? | **Hayır** |
| Reklam/analitik amaçlı kullanım? | **Hayır** |

> Not: takma ad sıralama tablosunda herkese açık görünür; bu, gizlilik
> politikasında açıkça belirtilmiştir.
